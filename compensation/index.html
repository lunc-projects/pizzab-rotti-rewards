Garuda cross-staking incident · 21 October 2026

ROTTI Staking Compensation Ledger

On 21 Oct 2026, Garuda's staking factory was used to drain its ROTTI cross-staking contract. Everyone who had ROTTI staked — or sitting in a pending unstake — lost it. This page is the on-chain record of exactly who is owed what, taken from the contract's own surviving stake records and reconciled to the last unit against the drain transaction.

What happened

  • 21 Oct 2026, 07:58:54 UTC — An unrelated wallet uploaded a malicious contract code (code 11735). Store-code tx
  • 07:59:06 UTC — The same wallet called migrate_staking on Garuda's staking factory. All 9 staking contracts were migrated to the malicious code and every balance was swept to the attacker's wallet in one transaction; the attacker began selling the tokens within minutes. Sweep tx
  • Drained ROTTI pool contract: terra13frf88v48egxhg5gtzmzzncag9wkxd00q76n65mqylg3hsnp3qrqsllpey · Attacker wallet: terra10ytpt4g5j3c0rmg2qlr6nyryqclcff87elszm6 (not a Garuda wallet — the factory let a wallet with no Garuda role migrate every staking contract).
  • Across all 9 drained contracts, 514,219,325,745 ROTTI was taken in total — the ROTTI pool above held most of it; the rest sat as ROTTI reward balances in other pools (for example the FUN pool).

How this was verified

The malicious code has no query functions, but it did not wipe the contract's storage. The contract's raw on-chain state was read after the attack; its stake records and pending-unstake records (wallet, amount, dates) survived intact.

Reconciled exactly: staked principal 365,414,557,239 ROTTI + pending unstakes 30,335,058,412 ROTTI + ROTTI rewards pool 91,780,694,292 ROTTI = 487,530,309,943 ROTTI — precisely what the sweep transaction took from this contract. Difference: 0.

Not included above: rewards that had accrued but not yet been claimed in this pool's cross-reward tokens. Those accrue per-staker from shared pools and cannot be split per wallet from balances alone; the surviving contract state holds the pool rates and last-withdraw timestamps needed to compute them.

ROTTI rewards, going forward

A standalone PIZZAB × ROTTI rewards app — separate from Garuda, with no migration backdoor of this kind — is live on this site: lunc-projects.github.io/pizzab-rotti-rewards. This ledger is only the record of the old Garuda pool; it does not move funds and is not a claim portal. Any compensation decision and funding rest with the Garuda and ROTTI teams.
